  1. Le lectiones del Mattutino secondo il Breviarium Ambrosianum
    Alessandro Re
    Ephemerides Liturgicae, 2026
    Abs

    The recensio of the biblical, patristic, and hagiographic pericopes contained in the 1957 edition of the "Breviarium Ambrosianum" – the last one before the reforms promoted by the Second Vatican Council – highlights several aspects of the Ambrosian tradition that merit further study. On the biblical side, one notes the substantial preservation of the ordo set out by Beroldus in the 12th century; the selection of patristic texts is marked by a prevalence of Ambrosian and Augustinian writings, though other authors are not absent; the lives of the saints, on the other hand, have undergone continual processes of updating in periods following the Council of Trent. The composite form of this document is a valuable historical witness to a multifaceted stratification, whose study may open the way to further investigations.

  2. Applications of Digital Analysis Techniques to Classical Languages and Literatures
    Alessandro Re
    Umanistica Digitale, 2026
    Abs DOI

    This article offers a survey of recent studies on the application of Natural Language Processing techniques to classical philology, with particular emphasis on Latin epic poetry of the 1st century AD. Although originally designed for the analysis of modern languages, the algorithms developed in computational linguistics over the past three decades – ranging from Latent Semantic Allocation and Latent Dirichlet Allocation to the more recent Transformer models based on Neural Networks – have transformed intertextual research through the practice of Topic Extraction. Particular attention is given to cases aimed at detecting situational correspondences even in the absence of co-occurring vocabulary. The examples discussed confirm both the efficacy of this approach and the convergence between ‘traditional’ philological methods and computational-linguistic techniques.

  2. Between Linguistics and Stylistics: Nominal Compounding and Literary Genre in Latin
    Renato Oniga and Alessandro Re
    In Exploring Latin: Structures, Functions, Meaning, 2025
    Abs DOI

    After having collected linguistic data from the 3rd century B.C. to the 5th century A.D., we worked out a typological classification of Latin nominal compounds based on a simplified Construction Grammar perspective. Then, we looked for links between the different types of compounds and the literary genre of the text in which they are used. The analysis shows that certain types of compounds (i.e. bahuvrīhi and most of nomina agentis) are mainly attested in high poetry, especially epics, while other types (i.e. nomina actionis and nominal abstracta) are primarily used in prose. This proves that, although morphological constructions are abstract entities whose existence in the linguistic competence of the speakers may only be supposed, they produce evident consequences in the stylistic nature of Latin literary texts.

  4. A graph database about Latin nominal compounds
    Andrea Brunello, Alessandro Re, and Giovanni Torresin
    Lingue antiche e moderne, 2025
    Abs DOI

    After collecting over the past years a substantial number of nominal compounds from Latin authors spanning from the archaic age to the late antiquity, a graph database has been created. This database is capable of classifying the types of compounds, showing the members they are composed of, and specifying in which works and by which authors they are used. The database can be interrogated through a series of queries that return structured data. The analysis of these data makes it possible to draw interesting insights not only about the specific occurrences of certain compounds within the examined corpus, but also to verify whether there are relationships linking literary genres to specific types of compounds.

  6. Tipologia e stilistica della composizione nominale latina
    Alessandro Re
    Classico Contemporaneo, 2024
    Abs

    Latin nominal composition is an aspect of linguistics that, over the centuries, has been approached through different paradigms in order to understand the underlying principles and its implications from a stylistic-philological point of view. In the 4th century B.C. Plato and Aristotle question the form and the value of nominal compounds, but only with Dionysius Thrax a formal classification of the names is proposed: in the Roman world, with the exception of Varro, Dionysius’ model is applied also to Latin without conceptual changes. Only by the knowledge of ancient Indian grammar we notice a significant change in the comprehension of this phenomenon, thanks to the introduction of a syntactic principle: thus Pāṇini’s model is applied also to Latin in order to improve the classification of nominal compounds. During the 20th century, structuralism and generative grammar redefine the entire question: in particular, through the construction grammar it is possible not only to identify the relationship between the shape of the compound and its meaning, but also to establish a link between typology and its stylistic value.

  1. Le origini latine dei verbi sintagmatici romanzi: osservazioni sull’uso dell’avverbio foras
    Paolo Lorusso, Renato Oniga, and Alessandro Re
    L’Analisi Linguistica e Letteraria, 2022
    Abs

    This article intends to investigate those Latin structures that prelude to Romance phrasal verbs. After having defined the object of study and having shown the Italian and Friulian data (§ 1), the various explanations of the phenomenon are presented, i.e. the Germanic hypothesis, the evolution within the framework of Greenberg’s typology, and the opposition between satellite-framed and verb-framed languages according to Talmy’s theory (§ 2). Then, the use of the adverb foras in Latin authors from the archaic period to late antiquity is analyzed in detail, trying to elaborate general lines of diachronic, sociolinguistic and stylistic variation (§ 3). Finally, a new interpretation of the constructions of movement verbs modified with prefixes, complements and adverbs is proposed, by means of the formalization of three lexico-sitnactic structures that follow one another along the entire span of the history of Latin (§ 4). The conclusion is that the last phase, although still compatible with classical Latin grammar, represents the premise for the development of Romance phrasal verbs (§ 5).

  2. Il plurilinguismo in Dante
    Alessandro Re
    In Cultura in Friuli VII. Settimana della cultura friulana - Setemane de culture furlane (18-28 ottobre 2020, 6-16 maggio 2021), 2022
    Abs

    De vulgari eloquentia is Dante’s unfinished Latin treatise on human linguistic faculties. Written at the beginning of the 14th century, it constitutes one of the earliest scientific reflections on the European vernacular languages. After identifying the origin of the plurality of languages in the confusion caused by God to halt the construction of the Tower of Babel, on the basis of the form of the affirmative adverb, Dante groups the European languages into the Germanic, Greek, and Romance branches. Within the latter group, he further distinguishes three varieties: the langue d’oc, the langue d’oïl, and the lingua di sì. Finally, with regard to the Italian peninsula, he surveys fourteen dialectal varieties which, to a considerable extent, can be compared with the linguistic areas identified by modern dialectological scholarship.

  3. Consonant epenthesis in Latin
    Renato Oniga and Alessandro Re
    Journal of Latin Linguistics, 2022
    Abs DOI

    This paper intends to focus on two aspects: how the Latin grammarians developed the concept of epenthesis, and how we can now give an explanation to this phenomenon. After having gathered the phonological evidence from the tradition of Latin philology, we propose that, in prehistoric Latin, the epenthesis of -t- in the -s.r- cluster, and the epenthesis of -p- in the -m.l- cluster, aimed at solving some problems in syllabification, that is avoiding an illicit syllable onset and an illicit coda-onset sequence, respectively. We then observe that, in classical Latin, the only productive process is the epenthesis of -p- in the -m.s- and -m.t- clusters, which has the function of fulfilling the faithfulness constraint on the inflection of the -m- stems, blocking the m \textgreater n assimilation process that later took place in the evolution from Latin to Italian.

  1. Ville in Plinio il Giovane, orizzonte reale o spazio ideale?
    Alessandro Re
    In Centro e periferia nella letteratura latina di Roma imperiale, 2021
    Abs

    The description of the villas and the activities there done runs through the entire correspondence of Pliny the Younger: this theme allows us to sketch an interesting analysis of society and culture of the Trajan era. The possession of extra-urban manors constitutes a real status symbol: Pliny provides significant information on their structure. They are the privileged place for the exercise of otia, among which literary activity plays the main role. In general, Pliny proposes a model of life marked by the search for moderation, that allows to gain that peace disturbed by the frenetic activities typical of Rome.

  2. Iam Christe tolle mentium. Note esegetiche tra mondo classico e cristiano
    Alessandro Re
    Zetesis, 2021
    Abs

    Iam Christe tolle mentium constitutes a peculiar Ambrosian recension of the hymn Iam Christe sol iustitiae, which is also well attested in the Breviarium Romanum. A careful analysis of the themes addressed in this brief composition reveals a rich intertextual network ranging from clear references to Roman religious culture, upon which the innovations of Christian preaching were grafted, to elements of the Jewish tradition. The lucid clarity of its exposition helps to define the themes of purification and asceticism characteristic of the Lenten season, which merge with the renewal of nature associated with spring.

  3. L’uso dei composti nominali nel Panegyricus Traiano Imperatori di Plinio il Giovane
    Alessandro Re
    Paideia, 2021
    Abs DOI

    In this work I deal with nominal composition in Pliny’s Panegyricus. Following Oniga’s tipology, first I classify each nominal compound recurring in that speech and then I propose their analysis, both in linguistic and literary terms. Also under this particular point of view, Pliny the Younger shows himself as a follower of “Ciceronian classicism” using a little number of compounds consacrated by an old tradition: no hapax legomena or neologisms recur. Finally I propose a comparison between the situation sketched in this work and those described by other scholars and myself with respect to other authors who have worked in earlier or later times (Julius Caesar, Sallust, Cicero, Cornelius Nepos, Varro, Tacitus, Petronius, Apuleius, Ammianus Marcellinus).

  5. La costruzione retorica dello straniero in Giovenale
    Alessandro Re
    Silvae di Latina Didaxis, 2021
    Abs

    Juvenal’s satires present various figures of foreigners – especially Orientals, Jews and Africans. The author’s attention is focused in order to denounce the intrinsic danger of their customs in relation to the Roman ones.   6. I composti nominali nel friulano
    Alessandro Re
    Ce fastu?, 2021
    Abs

    Nominal composition is one of the main means a language has to extend its lexicon through the combination of simple units into more complex forms. In the context of the Romance family, Friulian too exploited the potentialities of nominal composition to create new expressions aimed at indicating abstract or concrete elements whose definition was not possible through basic units. The structure and syntax of the six main types of compounds (coordinating, attributive, appositional, subordinating, prepositional, possessive) is examined on the synchronic level through the use of pertinent examples. More specifically, we examine how Friulian innovated itself in this aspect compared to Latin and how it was influenced by the Germanic and Slavonic languages spoken in the nearby territories.

  2. La terza declinazione latina: un approccio didattico basato sul metodo neo-comparativo
    Alessandro Re
    Rassegna Italiana di Linguistica Applicata, 2020
    Abs DOI

    Teaching Latin third declension poses larger problems than the other four. The classical partition between parisyllabic and unparisyllabic nouns has poor scientific value although it dates to Priscian the grammarian.   3. Genus compositicium. La composizione nominale latina
    Alessandro Re
    2020
    Abs

    The widespread assumption that the Latin language is inherently poor in nominal compounds is clearly challenged by a careful examination of the Latin authors. Starting with a historical investigation aimed at determining how linguistic scholarship, from the Hellenistic period to the present day, has accounted for the phenomenon of nominal compounding, this study identifies the tools of generative grammar as an exemplary model capable of scientifically explaining the rules by which simple words are combined to form lexemes with new meanings. Finally, it relates the typology of nominal compounds to the literary genres in which they are employed: both synchronically and diachronically, it emerges that certain compositional patterns have always been rare, whereas the productivity of others increases over time depending on the genre in which they occur.

  1. L’analyse synchronique des composés nominaux du latin hier et aujourd’hui
    Renato Oniga and Alessandro Re
    L’Antiquité Classique, 2017
    Abs DOI

    The study of nominal compounds in classical antiquity shows some similarities with contemporary linguistics, especially generative grammar. Ancient grammarians wished to develop a synchronic typology based on the inflectional features of the two members of the compound and the presence of the derivation. The introduction of syntactic criteria and the identification of the special characteristics of bahuvrīhi compounds are the main achievements of the Indian grammarians. Starting from a fundamentally Sanskrit typology, Latin linguistics in the 19th and 20th century attempted to develop new theories.

  1. Glossae Latino-Anglicae: il lessico ittico nei glossari anglosassoni
    Alessandro Re
    Lingue antiche e moderne, 2014
    Abs DOI

    This paper deals with the lexicon of aquatic animals in Anglo-Saxon as we can infer it from old English glossaries. A good number of glossaries dating back to the Middle Ages are preserved; in them we can find either Latin-Latin or bilingual glosses, in particular Latin-Old English. In this paper I will only focus on the latter group. In Section 1 (Introduction) I outline a sort of status quo in relation to the various typologies of documents containing Old English glosses, and discussing whether different works supposed distinct kind of users. The article also deals with the problem of sources of lemmata attested in the glossaries I studied. In Section 2 I briefly give the list of the glossaries I analysed and I focus on the dating of the texts and their edition. Section 3 contains the most important part of this paper: the Old English glosses to a large amount of Latin fishnames are gathered and classified. My main aim is discussing etymological problems with special regard to the other Germanic languages and possible links with Indo-European idioms, both from a diachronic and a synchronic point of view. The conclusion – in Section 4 – tries to sum up and give an overview of this lexical field.

  1. Genus compositicium. La composizione nominale in latino
    Alessandro Re
    2013
    Abs

    This dissertation examines nominal compounding in the Latin language. Using the tools of generative grammar, it is possible to define in an almost ‘mathematical’ way the patterns according to which Latin nominal and verbal roots can be combined to create a variety of neologisms, some of which are widespread in everyday use (e.g. agricola, beneficus, artifex), while others are deliberately recherché and poetically marked (e.g. Troiugena, frugiferens, flammicomus). An analysis of a group of Latin authors dating from the 1st century AD to Late Antiquity confirms that the distribution of compositional patterns and their use are closely connected to the literary genre in which they occur.

  1. Studio su alcuni composti in antico nordico
    Alessandro Re
    2010
    Abs

    Among the Germanic languages, Icelandic was – and in some respects still is – the language that has made the greatest use of nominal compounding as a means of enriching its vocabulary. This dissertation examines several semantic fields related to emotions, as expressed through nominal compounds formed with blóð “blood”, gall “bile”, milti “spleen”, and tár “tear.” In addition to determining whether the expressions under study can be related to the samāsa types of Old Indian, the dissertation investigates whether other ancient Germanic languages contain etymologically comparable expressions. Finally, it offers reflections on the formation of the Germanic poetic lexicon, also in relation to the broader tradition of the Indo-European peoples.

  1. Osservazioni su alcuni glossari latino-antico inglese
    Alessandro Re
    2007
    Abs

    Bilingual glossaries provide valuable evidence of the lexical enrichment of the Anglo-Saxon language from as early as the early Middle Ages, the period in which the earliest written records of Old English began to appear. This dissertation explores two distinct lexical fields – kinship relations and fish-related terminology – which clearly demonstrate that the numerous neologisms attested in these glossaries are not mere borrowings, but rather reflect various expressive techniques aimed at conveying as clearly and precisely as possible the meaning of each expression.
