In 2022, I held a research fellowship at the University of Udine, in collaboration with the Friulian Philological Society — [Società Filologica Friulana](https://www.filologicafriulana.it/).
During this period, I studied Friulian phrasal verbs, focusing in particular on the typological shift that occurred in the transition from Latin to the Romance languages.

Whereas Latin exhibits a clearly *verb-framed* typology, the Romance languages are generally characterized as *satellite-framed*.
Traces of this transition can already be identified in Latin, as demonstrated by the documentary evidence I collected.
This material was presented at an [online seminar](https://clt.uab.cat/activitats_clt/paolo-lorusso-renato-oniga-alessandro-re-le-origini-latine-dei-verbi-sintagmatici-romanzi/) held at the "Centre de Lingüística Teòrica" of the Universitat Autònoma de Barcelona in May 2022.

A revised version of the talk, prepared jointly with Paolo Lorusso and Renato Oniga, was subsequently published as an article in [L’analisi linguistica e letteraria](https://www.analisilinguisticaeletteraria.eu/index.php/ojs/article/view/508).
