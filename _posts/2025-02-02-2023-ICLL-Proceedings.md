In the last weeks, the [volume of proceedings](https://doi.org/10.1515/9783111332956) of the [22nd International Colloquium on Latin Linguistics](https://icll2023.ff.cuni.cz/programme/), held in Prague in June 2023, was published.

Renato Oniga and I participated presenting a paper titled [Between Linguistics and Stylistics: Nominal Compounding and Literary Genre in Latin](https://doi.org/10.1515/9783111332956-008).

Here is a brief summary of the contents of our talk.

The collection of linguistic data from the 3rd century B.C. to the 5th century A.D. made possible a typological classification of Latin nominal compounds based on a simplified [Construction Grammar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Construction_grammar) perspective.

This work showed evident links between the different types of compounds and the literary genre of the text in which they are used.
Certain types of compounds – i.e. *bahuvrīhi* and most of *nomina agentis* – are mainly attested in high poetry, and especially epics, while other types – i.e. *nomina actionis* and nominal *abstracta* – are primarily used in prose.

Essentially, although morphological constructions are abstract entities whose existence in the linguistic competence of the speakers may only be supposed, they produce evident consequences in the stylistic nature of Latin literary texts.
