In 2021, the seven-hundredth anniversary of Dante Alighieri's death prompted the [Società Filologica Friulana](https://www.filologicafriulana.it/) to devote a substantial part of the eight edition of the [Setemane de Culture Furlane](https://www.setemane.it/viii-edizione-6-16-maggio-2021/) to this author and his literary production.

Invited to contribute to a conference entitled "*Ces fastu crudeliter accentuando eructuant*. Dante e il Friuli settecento anni dopo", I examined whether and to what extent, the linguistic theories expressed in Dante's [De vulgari eloquentia](https://la.wikisource.org/wiki/De_Vulgari_Eloquentia) can still be regarded as relevant when considered in the light of recent developments in this field of research.

A revised version of my contribution was subsequently published in the [conference proceedings](https://hdl.handle.net/11390/1226297).
