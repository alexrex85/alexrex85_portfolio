In 2021, two of my contributions on the works of Pliny the Younger were published.
I have held a particular admiration for this author, owing not only to the depth and breadth of his subjects but also to the sophistication and refinement of his literary style.

With regard to the former aspect, I presented a paper entitled "Ville in Plinio il Giovane, orizzonte reale o spazio ideale?" at the international conference [Centro e periferia nella letteratura latina di età imperiale](https://www.letture.org/centro-e-periferia-nella-letteratura-latina-di-roma-imperiale-maria-luisa-delvigo), organized in January 2021 by the [DIUM of the University of Udine](https://dium.uniud.it/it/).
A revised version of the paper was subsequently published in the [conference proceedings](https://digital.casalini.it/10.1400/285544).

My interest in the linguistic dimension of Pliny's writing led me to investigate the nominal compounds employed in his [Panegyricus to Trajan](https://la.wikisource.org/wiki/Panegyricus_(Plinius)), one of the masterpieces of Latin oratory.
These lexical items are witness of the highly refined and sophisticated character of Pliny's diction, which constitutes one of the most distinctive features of his literary production.
Then, the results of this research were published in the prestigious journal [Paideia](https://digital.casalini.it/10.1400/285698).

Although the two studies originated from different epistemological perspectives, they both contribute to a deeper comprehension of the human and literary figure of Pliny the Younger.
