On the Feast of the Nativity of the Virgin Mary, I am delighted to present to the international scholarly community the first stage of a major research project devoted to the study and recensio of ecclesiastical hymns.

Entitled [Hymnarium Digitale](https://alexrex85.github.io/Hymnarium/index.html), the project reflects the dual nature of this undertaking.
On the one hand, it seeks to provide a comprehensive catalogue of hymnographic production, approached from both synchronic and diachronic perspectives.
On the other, by harnessing the potential of digital technologies, it aims to develop a dynamic infrastructure for the exploration and analysis of textual traditions.

The first resources now available to users include an [alphabetical catalogue of the *incipit*](https://alexrex85.github.io/Hymnarium/01_incipit_index.html) of the hymns collected so far, together with a [search interface](https://alexrex85.github.io/Hymnarium/02_versus_index.html) that makes it possible to conduct cross-referenced queries across textual data, sources, and authors.

Further tools and resources will be progressively made available over the coming months, as the project continues to develop.
